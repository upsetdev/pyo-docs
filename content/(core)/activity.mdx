## Activity

**Activity** records what happened across your team: who deployed or changed what, and when. Find it in the sidebar's **Observability** menu. You can also open it from the bell in the header with **View all activity**.

When an admin changes Pyo's own settings, such as observability, updates, backups or Tailscale, it shows in the activity of the team whose cluster runs Pyo.

Owners and admins can also see how and from where each change was made, with sign-ins included, in the [audit log](/docs/audit-log).

## Notifications

The bell in the header shows your team's events. Choose which topics appear under **Notifications**, in the sidebar's **Team** section:

- Failed deployments
- Successful deployments
- Server health
- Domains
- Service changes
- Team and projects

These topics are for this browser only. To tell the whole team in Telegram, Discord, Slack or a <Term tip="A web address that one system calls to tell another that something happened.">webhook</Term>, set up [alerts](/docs/alerts).
