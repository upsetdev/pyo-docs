## Activity

**Activity** records what happened across your team: who deployed or changed what, and when. Find it in the sidebar's **Observability** menu. You can also open it from the bell in the header with **View all activity**.

## Notifications

The bell in the header shows your team's events. Choose which topics appear under **Notifications**, in the sidebar's **Team** section:

- Failed deployments
- Successful deployments
- Server health
- Domains
- Service changes
- Team and projects

Notifications are in the dashboard only for now. Email, Slack and <Term tip="A web address that one system calls to tell another that something happened.">webhooks</Term> aren't available yet.
