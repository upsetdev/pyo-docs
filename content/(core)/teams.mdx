Everything in Pyo belongs to a team, and you can be in several.

## Roles

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Full control, including deleting the team |
| Admin | Manage members, clusters and every project |
| Developer | Deploy and configure services |
| Viewer | Read-only access |

## Invite people

Sign-ups are closed, so people join by invitation. Invite them by email under **Members**, in the sidebar's **Team** section. With [email](/docs/email) set up, the invite goes straight to them. Without it, you get a link to share yourself.

People join through the link. Signing up with the invited address isn't enough. The link works once, for 14 days, and only for an account with the invited address. Someone who already has an account with that address is added right away. **Resend invite** and **Copy invite link** make a new link, and the old one stops working.

## API keys and assistants

[API keys](/docs/api) and [AI assistants](/docs/mcp) act as the person who created or approved them, with that person's role.
