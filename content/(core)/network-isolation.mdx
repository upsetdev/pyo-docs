---
title: Isolating projects
description: Keep projects from reaching each other.
---

New projects are isolated. Only their own services, [Pyo Gateway](/docs/gateway), public ports and your team's [database imports](/docs/databases#import-data) can reach them.

To open a project to the rest of the cluster, or isolate it again, go to **Security** in the sidebar's **Infrastructure** section. When a project is open, any service in the cluster can reach it.

To expose a service on purpose, see [other kinds of traffic](/docs/domains#other-kinds-of-traffic). To choose who can reach it from outside, use the [firewall](/docs/firewall).
