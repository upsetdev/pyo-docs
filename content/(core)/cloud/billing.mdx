## The price

- **$5 per server per month.** Each connected server counts once, whatever it runs.
- **20% off** when you pay yearly.
- **Everything included.** Clusters, projects, services, databases and users are unlimited. There are no request or traffic limits.
- **No trial.** Self-hosted Pyo is free if you want to try it first. See [Installing Pyo](/docs/install).

## Paying

Open **Settings → Billing** and add a payment method. A team needs one before its first server connects.

- **Card,** anywhere, through Polar. It renews by itself. Polar sends invoices and handles sales tax and VAT.
- **QRIS, bank transfer or e-wallet,** in Indonesia, through Midtrans. You pay in rupiah, a month or a year at a time.

Your bill follows your servers:

- **Adding a server** charges for the rest of the month.
- **Removing a server** credits your next invoice.

## When a payment fails

Your services keep running. After 7 days, your plan pauses until you pay. **Settings → Billing** says when.

## While a plan is paused or ended

Your team's servers get no changes. Deploys and other changes are refused with a message that says why.

- Your services keep running.
- You can still see everything, read logs and delete things.

Paying resumes your plan at once. Pyo then sends the updates it held back, like routes, firewall rules and cron jobs.

## Canceling

Cancel anytime from **Settings → Billing**. Your plan ends at the end of the period you paid for. Removing all your servers cancels it the same way.

After it ends, your services keep running on your servers. To remove what Pyo put there, see [Disconnect a cluster](/docs/uninstall#disconnect-a-cluster).

Moving to a Pyo you run yourself? See [Moving to self-hosted](/docs/cloud/moving-to-self-hosted).
