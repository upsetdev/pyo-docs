## The price

- **$5 per server per month.** Each connected server counts once, whatever it runs.
- **20% off** when you pay yearly.
- **Everything included.** Clusters, projects, services, databases and users are unlimited.
- **No trial.** Self-hosted Pyo is free if you want to try it first. See [Installing Pyo](/docs/install).

## Paying

Open **Settings → Billing** and add a payment method. A team needs one before its first server connects.

- **Card,** anywhere, through Polar. It renews by itself. Polar sends invoices and handles sales tax and VAT.
- **QRIS, bank transfer or e-wallet,** in Indonesia, through Midtrans. You pay in rupiah, a month or a year at a time.

Your bill follows your servers:

- **Adding a server** charges for the rest of the month.
- **Removing a server** credits your next invoice.

## When a payment fails

Your services keep running. After 7 days, deploys pause until you pay. **Settings → Billing** says when.

## Canceling

Cancel anytime from **Settings → Billing**. Your services keep running on your servers. To remove what Pyo put there, see [Disconnect a cluster](/docs/uninstall#disconnect-a-cluster).
