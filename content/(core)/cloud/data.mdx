Your visitors' requests stay on your servers. Pyo Cloud keeps only totals.

## On your servers

- **Every request,** in compressed files on a small volume in your cluster. The last 7 days or 1 GB by default, whichever comes first. A team setting changes both.
- **Visitors' IP addresses.** Countries and the daily [visitor hash](/docs/observability#analytics) are worked out on your servers, so IP addresses never leave them.

**Request logs** and their live view ask your cluster through the agent, so they need the cluster online.

## On Pyo Cloud

Totals for each service and domain, per minute:

- requests by status and method, and bytes sent
- response times
- visitors
- countries, top pages and referrers
- requests the [firewall](/docs/firewall) and [WAF](/docs/waf) blocked

Totals are kept for 12 months. **Analytics** reads only totals, so it works while a cluster is offline.

## Everything else

- **Build logs** are kept for 90 days.
- **Service logs** come from your servers live, when you open them.
- **Backups** go from your servers straight to your own bucket. See [Backups](/docs/cloud/differences#backups).
- **Your services' data,** databases and volumes stay on your servers.
