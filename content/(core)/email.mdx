---
title: Setting up email
description: Send invites through your own SMTP server.
---

Pyo sends email through an <Term tip="The server that sends email for you. Your email provider gives you its host, port and login.">SMTP server</Term> you choose. An installation admin sets it up under **Settings → Email**. Enter the host, port, encryption, username, password and sender address. You can send a test email from the same page.

For now, email is only used for team invites. Notifications aren't sent by email yet.
