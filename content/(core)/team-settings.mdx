---
title: Configuring your team
description: Name your team and set its defaults.
---

Find these under **Settings**, in the sidebar's **Team** section:

- **Team name and slug:** the <Term tip="A short, URL-friendly version of the name, like acme-inc.">slug</Term> appears in your dashboard URLs.
- **Default target cluster:** where new deploys go.

Deleting a team removes its projects and clusters from Pyo. Nothing on the clusters is touched.
